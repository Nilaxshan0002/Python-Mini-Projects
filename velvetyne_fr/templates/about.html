About Velvetyne

Velvetyne is an independent type foundry founded in 2010. We create retail and custom fonts that are expressive, versatile, and made to last.

Our goal is to provide designers with unique typographic tools that push the boundaries of traditional design while remaining highly functional. Every font we release is carefully crafted with attention to detail and a strong personality.
